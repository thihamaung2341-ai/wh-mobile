Shopping with WH Mobile

What to know before you order

Check the current price and availability with our team, and confirm the details that matter to you before placing an order.

Product details

Listings show the specifications currently available for each phone. Ask us to confirm any detail that is missing, including condition, color, battery health, known defects, or included accessories.

Price and availability

Prices and stock can change. If a listing says “Price updated daily,” message us for today’s price and to confirm the phone is still available.

Delivery and pickup

Choose standard delivery or store pickup at checkout. WH Mobile will confirm any delivery fee before fulfilling your order.

Payment

Payment arrangements are confirmed by WH Mobile before fulfilment. Contact us if you want to confirm accepted payment methods before ordering.

Warranty

Warranty coverage and terms vary by product. Please confirm the applicable coverage and duration for the specific phone before purchase.

Returns and exchanges

Please contact us before purchasing to confirm whether the specific item is eligible and what conditions apply.

Need to check something?

Our team can confirm current product details, price, stock, delivery, and after-sales terms.
